What is stored, and why

Scans you run while signed in are stored against your account: the URL, the assessment, the risk score, the model version and the time. This is what makes history and analytics possible.

The URL is never fetched. Assessment is performed on the URL text alone. No request is made to the address, so no DNS resolution, IP, domain age or reputation data is collected — those fields read Not available by design, not by failure.

Passwords are stored only as Argon2id hashes and are never logged. Refresh tokens are stored hashed, so a copy of the database does not yield usable sessions.

Clearing your history deletes those rows permanently.
